RyBot privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026 · Terms · Support

What RyBot is

RyBot is a private assistant run by the Fidler family on a computer in our home. It is not a public service: only family members invited by the owner can sign in.

What data it uses

When a family member connects a Google account, RyBot can read and act on that person's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, only to do what that person (or a parent, for a child's helpers) asks. Each person connects their own account and can disconnect it at any time.

Where it is kept

Messages, connected-account tokens and files stay on the family's own computer at home. Tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train AI models by RyBot.

To answer a request, RyBot may send the relevant text to the AI model the family has chosen for that helper, which may be a model running at home or an AI provider such as Anthropic. Google user data is used only to provide the feature the person asked for.

Google user data

RyBot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Disconnect your Google account in RyBot under your profile, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Ask the owner to delete your RyBot account and its history.
