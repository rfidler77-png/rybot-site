RyBot support

Last updated October 21, 2026 · Privacy · Terms

What RyBot is

RyBot is a private family assistant. The helpers run on a computer in the family's own home; the RyBot app lets family members talk to them from their phone.

Getting access

RyBot only works with a household's own RyBot server. To join, ask the person who set it up at home for an invite, then open the invite link or enter its code in the app.

Troubleshooting

Can't connect: check your internet connection and try again. If it still fails, ask the household's owner for a new invite.

No notifications: open iPhone Settings → Notifications → RyBot and turn on Allow Notifications.

A helper is waiting for a parent: purchases from a child's helper need a parent's approval in the app.
